Before you call

The questions every first-time buyer asks us.

Do I really pay nothing extra?

On new launches, yes — the developer pays our commission and your price is the same as walking in directly (often better, because we negotiate for you). On resale, standard brokerage applies and we tell you the number upfront.

Will you push the projects that pay you more?

Our advisors are not paid per project. Every verdict on our site lists what we didn't like, too. If none of our partner projects fit, we'll say so.

Can you help with the home loan?

Yes — we work with [X] partner banks and NBFCs, compare rates for you and handle the paperwork. Pre-approval usually takes 3–5 working days.

What if the project gets delayed?

We only list RERA-registered projects, and we show you the developer's delivery history before you book. If a delay happens, we help you with the RERA process.
