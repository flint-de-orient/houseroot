How HouseRoot works

From your first call to the keys in your hand, one advisor stays with you. Here is what the next few weeks look like.

  1. Step 1Tell us your brief

    A 15-minute call. Budget, localities, timeline, what matters: schools, metro, Vastu, rental yield.

  2. Step 2Get a shortlist of 3 to 5

    Only projects our team has walked, compared on price per sq ft, carpet area, hidden charges and delay risk.

  3. Step 3Visit, then negotiate

    Free pick-up and drop across Kolkata, weekends included. We use our developer relationships to get the best offer.

  4. Step 4Book and move in

    Zero brokerage on new homes. We stay for the loan, agreement review, registration and a possession snag-list.
